About Kahani

Kahani by ShareYourGroup is a free collection of 20 short stories in Hindi for children of about four to eleven years: stories with a lesson, animal stories, funny stories, family stories and stories of school and friendship. Every story comes with its moral, three questions to talk about and three new words explained in simple Hindi and English.

Where the stories come from

Every story on this site is original and was written for this site, in everyday Hindi. They are new stories with Indian homes, villages and schools as their setting. None is a retelling of the Panchatantra, Hitopadesha, Jataka tales, Akbar and Birbal, Tenali Rama or any other collection, and nothing is taken from a book, a film, a textbook or another website. Names of people and places are made up. The English neighbour of this site, stories.shareyourgroup.com, has a different set of stories; none is repeated here.

Reading aloud

The "सुनिए" button uses the Hindi voice built into your phone or computer, if it has one. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Language note

The Hindi was written with care, with a few common Urdu-origin words spelt with the nukta (ज़, फ़, ख़, क़) as many Hindi publishers do. It has not yet been read by a second native speaker. If a word or a line reads wrongly, please tell us. Last reviewed in November 2026.
